WordNext Photo app icon (photocraft.svg, photocraft-small.svg and every file derived from them in
this directory: photocraft-1024.png, photocraft.icns, photocraft.ico, hicolor/).

The WordNext monogram: original artwork by WordNext (the mark of wordnext.tech), replacing the
upstream PhotoCraft icon in this fork. No third-party material. The icon is a WordNext trademark:
forks and modified versions of WordNext Photo must replace it.

The files were rendered from the monogram's SVG (photocraft.svg) at 16 to 1024 px; the .ico and
.icns containers were assembled from those PNGs.

The upstream PhotoCraft icon (the nine-tailed kitsune) is not part of this fork; see the upstream
repository for its terms.
